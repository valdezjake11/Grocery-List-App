01 · Overview

Make grocery planning easier.

The Smart Grocery List App is a student mobile-app project focused on making everyday shopping lists easier to create and manage. Users can add grocery items, update quantities, organize products into categories, search their list, and mark items as purchased. Data is stored locally on the device using AsyncStorage.

02 · Key features

What the app can do

🛒

Manage grocery items

Add, edit, and delete items, including their quantities and categories.

✓

Track purchases

Mark items as purchased and view your completed-item count.

🔎

Search and filter

Find items quickly and filter the list by grocery category.

💾

Save your list

Keep grocery-list data on the device with local storage.

📊

View list progress

See total items and completed items at a glance.

🧹

Clear completed items

Remove purchased items when you want to tidy your list.
